9. **Waiting for `demo-faults`**: a call waits for the earlier one without a bound (the fair lock); its own timeout is the time left to the limit, at least 0.5 s.
10. **Logging**: `Reachability` logs nothing (not required); operation lines carry the times.

## 4. Document feedback (CTX-Q-8)

1. **AR-B-03 / spec 6.4 item 6, the settle time at the limit**: the complete list of error texts has none for "the lab became quiet, but the 12 s settle time would end after the 58 s limit". Section 3 item 5 uses "Lab alerts were still firing after 58 s: Zabbix 0, Alertmanager 0.", which reads oddly. A text such as "The lab became quiet only {s} s before the limit." (or letting the settle time run past the limit) is the owner's choice.
2. **trace.py and part 4**: five part-4 IDs had no test name in Section 7 (Section 3 item 1); later specs MAY list a test name for every part-4 ID.
3. **The JRE image has no `curl` or `wget`**: the compose health check of `demo-control` uses bash's `/dev/tcp`; P-32's main compose file needs the same.
4. **SB-T-05's 40 s resolve target does not hold reliably in the cloud `test` profile, and the spec's assumption of Section 11 fails** (CTX-D-068 (3)). The quiet times measured by "Clear all faults" and reset branch A after a storm in this session: **38.027 s**, then **40.036 s**, **40.027 s** and **40.029 s** (the last three after SL-F-1 + SL-F-3, SL-F-1 + SL-F-3 and SL-F-1 + SL-F-2 + SL-F-4). With reads every 2 s (AR-B-03) the measured quiet time is the first 2 s step after the lab became quiet plus the read itself (about 30 ms), so 40.03 s means the lab became quiet between 38.0 and 40.0 s — the margin CTX-D-068 (3) asked to watch is under 2 s and below the measurement's resolution. The check `quiet_seconds ≤ 40` (spec 6.8 item 3) then fails on the 30 ms read time, and `e2e.yml`'s step "control panel" will fail the same way. Likewise "Clear all faults completed in 52 s" was shown for 52.181 s and 52.126 s (whole seconds rounded down, DC-S12-05), so "≤ 52" passes on the text although the clear took slightly longer than 52 s. The owner decision CTX-D-068 (3) names (the target, the trigger's 20 s window, or the clear wait), or measuring the quiet time at the start of the quiet read, is needed; this package changes none of them.
