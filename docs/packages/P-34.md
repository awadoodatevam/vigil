## 2. Goal

Make the primary demo (SB-S00 to SB-S10) ready to be shown and prove SL-CP-2 on the demo installation. Three changes are built: `vigil-app` sends `vigil-web`'s built files gzip-compressed (AR-W-01 as amended by BP-A-33; the 321 KB script took 12 to 52 s over the owner's link in P-32); the demo control panel (DC-S12) takes Vigil's tokens and fonts, with no new text or behaviour (`45-visual-design` v1.4, the SL-CP-1 change request of CTX-D-096 (5)); and the acceptance tooling that runs and judges SB-T-02's five runs, SB-T-04's step times, the RB-DB-01 citation count and the rehearsals. Then the owner proves, through the public names: five storm driver runs in a row from a reset in mode `script` with every exact value of SB-S01 to SB-S09 and the Playwright suite `storm` beside each, the dashboard on the demo host included (SB-T-02, SL-A3-04, SB-T-03 in the product); the step times (SB-T-04) and the local model's SL-T-1 answers under PM-M17-03; two rehearsals with the senior manager within 15:00 and every scene within ±30 s (SB-T-08); and the backup video of the primary demo (SL-A3-08). A result that fails is fixed in a fix round (Section 6.9): a product defect against a document, a test defect, or the SL-T-1 rewording that AR-G-12 allows. The package ends with the SL-CP-2 review (Section 8.3).

### 6.10 Fix round 1 (27 Nov 2026; CTX-D-099 (2), DC-S03-07, AR-W-02)

1. **What failed.** The first series (`tests/acceptance/results/P-34-series-20261127T052633Z.txt`, release `p34-1`): all five driver runs passed 33 of 33 checks (RB-DB-01 cited in 5 of 5, summary lengths 439 to 529, step times within SB-T-04's limits); the suite `storm` passed completely in run 1, and in runs 2 to 5 it stopped at P-29's "SL-A1-08 the highlight followed every step change within 2 s": `s-ticket`, `s-notify` and `s-wait-ack` were highlighted 2.1 to 7.3 s after they started (run 3 up to 7.3 s for `s-similar`), against DC-S03-07's 2 s. The server's step times were the same in all five runs.
2. **The cause** (Claude's reading of run 2's Playwright trace, 27 Nov 2026): during the burst of step changes after the incident leaves `forming` (`s-diagnose` to `s-wait-ack` within about 10 s), each notice of `run:{id}` and `incident:{id}` re-reads every query of the incident's workspace, and `liveHandlers` calls `invalidateQueries`, which by default cancels a read that is still running. Over the owner's link the workspace's largest answers (the alerts section about 77 KB and the timeline about 42 KB, both uncompressed JSON) take 1 to 2 s, so every batch of re-reads was cancelled by the next notice (in the trace, every batch started between 05:39:10.66 and 05:39:14.48 ended without an answer) and the run's own small read (about 7.5 KB, 0.5 s) completed only once the notices paused: `s-ticket`, started 05:39:10.129, was drawn at 05:39:15.02. The server and the stream were not slow (round trip about 240 ms; notices arrived). Run 1 passed because its notices fell differently.
3. **Fix A** (`web/src/live/useLiveTopics.ts`, AR-W-02): a notice never cancels a read that is running: `liveHandlers`' `invalidate` and `invalidateAll` invalidate with `cancelRefetch: false`; when a notice arrives while a query's read runs, that query is read once more as soon as the running read ends, so the screen always ends on a read that started after the last notice (at most one extra read per query, whatever the number of notices meanwhile). AR-TM-04's "at most once per 500 ms per topic" is unchanged. New Vitest tests in `web/src/live/` (complete list): "AR-W-02 a notice during a running read does not cancel it"; "AR-W-02 a notice during a running read leads to exactly one more read after it"; "AR-W-02 a notice with no read running reads at once".
4. **Fix B** (AR-W-01 as amended, BP-A-33): `application/json` joins `server.compression.mime-types` (Section 6.2 item 1), so JSON answers of 2 KB or more are sent gzip-compressed (the alerts section and the timeline shrink to a few KB); the live stream, the error answers, the fonts and the actuator stay uncompressed. Test: `WebAppServingIT`'s changed test of Section 7.
5. **Report**: a section "Fix round 1" with the cause, both fixes, their tests, `tools/loc.sh`, and the first series' results (per run: the driver's checks, the suite `storm`'s result and the highlight delays printed by SL-A1-08).
6. **Then the owner** builds `p34-2` on the Mac and runs Section 6.8 steps 2 to 6 again (a new series of five on `p34-2`); step 7 (the local model) runs once on any release, since the SL-T-1 system text is unchanged. The first series' files stay in the repository as the record of this round; its checklists need not be ticked.
