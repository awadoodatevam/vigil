## 2. Goal

Build the two mobile pages that the links in messages open (SL-A4-04, SL-A4-05): the Acknowledge page `/m/acknowledge/{incident id}` (DC-S04) and the Approval page `/m/approve/{approval request id}` (DC-S05), in one column usable at 360 px (AR-W-07), with their refusals, the countdown, the 1 s polling and the live state, and the confirmation dialog of DC-U-11 for Approve and Reject. They read and act through AR-I-32 to AR-I-35 (P-43) and look as `45-visual-design` v1.2 says (VD-S-06, VD-S-07). The mobile pages load only their own code (AR-W-07). The package also builds the three items CTX-D-080 (4) carried here: one team membership read in `acknowledge` and `decide`, a repeated member in AR-I-35's body refused with 400, and an AR-I-27 test with a run of two approval requests. Playwright proves the pages at 360 px during a storm driver run in which, in mode `immediate`, the pages themselves acknowledge and approve (the driver's new option `--acts pages`, CTX-D-091 (7)).

### 6.3 Page states, permissions and refusals (DC-U-05, DC-U-09, DC-S04-01, DC-S04-05, DC-S05-01, DC-S05-06)

1. **States of the read.** While AR-I-32 or AR-I-34 has not answered: `Loading` in a card. A `403` answer (the user holds neither the action's permission nor `view`, AR-I-07): in place of the content card, the warning notice "You do not have permission to acknowledge incidents." or "You do not have permission to approve actions." (DC-S04-01, DC-S05-01, VD-C-26). Any other failure, `404` included (an unknown, `forming` or `merged` incident; an unknown request): `PageError` "Could not load this page. {reason}" with "Try again" (for a 404 the reason is "Not found."). A failed re-read after an answer shows `PageError` in place of the page (P-28's reading).
2. **The action area** (VD-C-26), from the latest answer, in this order (complete list): (a) the outcome of this page's own action (Section 6.4 item 2, Section 6.5 item 5) while the latest answer's refusal is `null` or the one that action caused (`already_acknowledged`, `already_decided`); (b) otherwise the refusal text (item 3) when `refusal` is not `null`, in place of the buttons (DC-S04-05, DC-S05-06: the first that applies); (c) otherwise, after this page's decision was refused `snapshot_changed` and until the page is reloaded, that refusal's message in place of the buttons (Section 6.5 item 3); (d) otherwise, after this page's action answered another 422 and the re-read carried no refusal, that 422's `message` in place of the button(s), until an answer carries a refusal (Section 6.4 item 2, Section 6.5 item 3); (e) otherwise, when `may_act` is true, the button "Acknowledge" or the buttons "Approve" and "Reject"; (f) "View only." whenever `may_act` is false (under the refusal when there is one) (DC-S04-01, DC-S05-01; a caller without the permission gets no personal refusal, CTX-D-079 (2)). The lines under the buttons ("You do not have permission to do this.", "Could not … {reason}") stay until the next press of a button of the page.
3. **Refusal texts** (`web/src/mobile/refusals.ts`, pure; CTX-D-079 (1), CTX-D-091 (4)): the three refusals whose text holds a time are composed with the viewer time from the fields the answer carries (complete list): `already_acknowledged` → "Already acknowledged by {acknowledged.user.display_name} at {viewer time of acknowledged.at}."; `already_decided` → "Already {approved or rejected} by {decided.user.display_name} at {viewer time of decided.at}." with the word from `state`; `expired` → "This request expired at {viewer time of expires_at}.". Every other refusal (`not_open`, `not_notified`, `cancelled`, `not_pending`, `not_eligible`, `excluded`, `snapshot_changed`, any other key) is shown as the answer's `message`, which is the text of DC-S04-05 or DC-S05-06.

### 6.6 Confirmation dialog (DC-U-11, VD-C-27)

`web/src/dialog/ConfirmDialog.tsx` (props: `question`, `confirmLabel`, `tone: "primary" | "destructive"`, `open`, `onConfirm`, `onCancel`): a native `<dialog>` opened with `showModal()` when `open` becomes true, `aria-labelledby` its question, "Cancel" first in the markup and focused through a ref right after `showModal()`, the confirming button styled by `tone`; `close()` when `open` becomes false. The `cancel` event (Escape) and "Cancel" call `onCancel`; the confirming button calls `onConfirm`. It is the component P-31, P-37 and P-39 use for their rows of DC-U-11.
