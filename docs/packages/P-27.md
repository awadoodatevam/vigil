## 2. Goal

Run the primary demo headless on the local installation and check its result (SB-T-02, BP-A-11): a storm driver that resets as AR-B-03 does, injects the storm, acknowledges as `noc-l1` and approves as `dbops-engineer` through links it builds from the identifiers it reads and compares with the links in the delivered messages, waits until the storm incident is resolved, and checks the SB-R-06 result and the values of SB-S01 to SB-S09 that need no screen, reading only `vigil-api`, the dev fakes, Mailpit and the lab's monitoring. Mode `immediate` acts as soon as possible and checks the times by the formulas of DM-R-53; mode `script` follows the script clock and checks the exact values. A wrapper script runs it in the compose profile `acceptance` and then checks the application log for test secrets (AR-Q-07). `e2e.yml` runs it in mode `immediate`; the owner runs mode `script` on the Mac.

**The run (complete list of steps; `T0` is the driver's UTC clock just before step 4's first request)**:

1. **Reset (AR-B-03, CTX-D-083 (3)).** Two threads at once. **(A)** `POST /faults/clear` with `{"faults": ["SL-F-1", "SL-F-2", "SL-F-3"]}` and the `faults-token` (timeout: the time left of the branch); then every 2 s until the lab is quiet: Zabbix's `problem.get` with the tag `vigil` = `lab` (bearer `zabbix-api`) returns no row, and no entry of Alertmanager's `/api/v2/alerts` (basic user `vigil` with `alertmanager-password`) has a `labels.alertname` among the five catalogue names of `03-monitoring.yaml`: NodeFilesystemFull, BalanceApiErrors, BundleApiErrors, UssdGatewayTimeouts, SyntheticUssdSessionFailed (as `storm_timing.py` reads them, without its `trigger.get` and catalogue); the time from the clear's answer to the first quiet read is recorded as `reset.quiet_seconds`; then 12 s. **(B)** AR-I-95 with `{"name": "jazz-demo {UTC time as YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ}"}`; AR-I-96 with the multipart field `bundle` holding `/run/state/bundle.zip` (the bundle `deploy/local.sh up` or `reset` built from the values file it used, CTX-D-083 (3)); AR-I-97 every 1 s until `succeeded` or `failed`; a state other than `succeeded`, or a part whose `outcome` is not `succeeded`, fails the branch. An answer of `demo-faults`, AR-I-95 or AR-I-96 other than the operation's success status fails its branch at once (AR-B-03); a failed branch fails the run without AR-I-98. Branch A as a whole takes at most 180 s from its start, branch B at most 300 s from its start; a branch at its limit fails. When both succeeded: AR-I-98 for the host of `VIGIL_BASE_URL` with the new tenant. The installation token is read from `/run/secrets/vigil-app/installation-token`. `state/tenant.json` and `state/seed-import.json` are not changed.
2. **Starting state (SB-SET-07, AR-B-04).** Log in the three users; every 2 s, at most 90 s, until AR-I-20 answers `sources.healthy` equal to `sources.total`. Then the starting checks of Section 6.3. Record, for deliveries `fakes`, the number of calls `GET http://teams.fakes:8000/_fakes/calls` returns and the `ID`s of Mailpit's messages (`GET /api/v1/messages?limit=1000`); only later calls and messages count in the delivery checks.
3. **Script start.** In mode `script` the storm is injected at once (T0 is the script's 01:00).
4. **Storm (SB-S01 operator).** `POST /faults/SL-F-1/inject`, then `POST /faults/SL-F-3/inject` (timeout 180 s each); any answer other than 200 fails the run.
5. **Incidents.** Every 2 s read AR-I-21 (`limit=50`) until it holds two rows, the storm incident (title starting `Subscriber DB server: `) and the report incident (title starting `Report export job: `), at most until T0 + 360 s. Their `incident_id`s are the identifiers of the links (AR-I-21, BP-A-11).
6. **Acknowledge (SB-S05).** Wait (every 2 s, at most until T0 + 420 s) until AR-I-29 of the storm incident shows the step `s-notify` `completed`, and with deliveries `fakes` until the Teams fake has a call on the path `/teams-team-noc-l1` carrying the storm incident's Acknowledge line. Build the link `{VIGIL_BASE_URL}/m/acknowledge/{storm incident id}` (AR-I-101 with the local link base, BP-A-09). Mode `script`: wait until T0 + 390 s (wall clock 07:30, inside SB-TL-06's 07:15 to 07:45); when the wait above ends after T0 + 405 s, the script cannot be followed and the run ends with the entry `SB-T-02` "the script can be followed: Acknowledge link by 07:45" not passed (a run abort, not a timing check, CTX-D-083 (4)). As `noc-l1`: AR-I-32 (the check `SB-S05 acknowledge page`), AR-I-33 (201), AR-I-32 again. The acknowledgement time is the `at` of AR-I-33's answer.
7. **Approve (SB-S06).** Every 2 s, at most 180 s after the acknowledgement (mode `script`: at most until T0 + 510 s, wall clock 09:30), read AR-I-27 of the storm incident until `approvals` holds a request in state `pending`, and with deliveries `fakes` until the Teams fake has a call on `/teams-user-dbops-engineer` carrying its Approve line. Build `{VIGIL_BASE_URL}/m/approve/{approval request id}` (AR-I-102). Mode `script`: wait until T0 + 480 s (wall clock 09:00); when the request or its message is not there by T0 + 510 s, the run ends with the entry `SB-T-02` "the script can be followed: Approval link by 09:30" not passed. As `dbops-engineer`: AR-I-34 (the check `SB-S06 approval page`), then AR-I-35 with `{"decision": "approve", "snapshot_hash_shown": snapshot_hash}` (201).
8. **Recovery (SB-S07).** Every 2 s, at most 480 s after the decision, read AR-I-22 of the storm incident until `state` is `resolved`; then every 2 s, at most 120 s from the resolution, read the ticket's status (Section 6.4) until it is `Done` (`resolved_status` of `07-settings.yaml`) and AR-I-29 of the storm incident until `run.state` is `finished` (the ticket is moved before the run ends).
9. **Checks.** The end checks of Section 6.3 (after step 8, or after the failing step for the checks whose data exists), the result file (Section 6.4), the exit code. The driver leaves SL-F-3 active and the report incident open, as the demo does (CTX-D-083 (7)); the next reset clears them.

A wait that reaches its limit ends the run: the result file gets `finished: false` and the entry `SB-T-02` "step {k} {step name} finished within {limit} s" not passed (`{k}` the step's number above, `{step name}` its bold name, `{limit}` the limit of the wait that ran out in seconds after its own start, or "T0 + N" where this section writes it so; Example: "step 6 Acknowledge finished within T0 + 420 s"), and the exit code is 1. A script abort (steps 6 and 7) likewise sets `finished: false`, adds only its "script can be followed" entry and exits 1. A connection error is retried by the wait loops; outside them it fails the run with its message.

### 6.4 Details of the driver

- **Message parsing.** A Teams call's `body` (the request text, parsed with `json.loads`) is the Adaptive Card of `TeamsConnector.payload`: its lines are the `text` of `attachments[0].content.body[*]`; the first line is the message's first line; a link line is `{label}[{L}]({L})` with the label `Acknowledge: ` or `Approve or reject: `, and both `{L}` must be equal. A Mailpit message (`GET /api/v1/message/{ID}`) has `To[0].Address`, the `Subject` (the first line) and `Text`, whose lines (`splitlines()`, the body is joined with CRLF) include `Acknowledge: {L}` or `Approve or reject: {L}`. The recipient of a Teams call is its path; the incident a message belongs to is the one whose link it holds.
- **Jira status.** `GET {base}/rest/api/3/issue/{key}?fields=status`, where `{base}` is the ticket `url` without `/browse/{key}`, with `Authorization: Basic base64({email}:{api_token})` of the `jira` secret (the fake ignores it; the real Jira of P-32 needs it).
- **DM-R-53 (`SB-S09 dashboard formulas`).** Counted incidents: the two live incidents and the 50 rows of the CSV. Time to acknowledge: the mean, over counted incidents with an acknowledgement, of the time from detection to the first acknowledgement, as `reporting/Metrics.compute` does it: differences in integer milliseconds (`acknowledged_after_minutes` × 60,000 for a CSV row; `acknowledged.at` − `first_alert_fired_at` of AR-I-22 for a live one, API times with milliseconds and `Z`), mean = their sum / (60,000 × n), rounded half-up to one decimal place. Time to recover: the same with `recovered_after_minutes` and `resolved_at`. Alerts suppressed: the CSV's `alert_count` sum plus the live incidents' `alert_count` (every alert the new tenant received belongs to one of the two incidents), minus the counted incidents (the live incidents with a number and the 50 rows: 52). Incidents merged: the counted incidents with an alert count of 2 or more. Means are computed with `decimal.Decimal`; AR-I-80 is parsed with `json.loads(…, parse_float=Decimal)` so that values compare exactly. Applied to the CSV alone, the formulas give DD-K-11's 5.7, 41.1, 525 and 46 (tested).
- **Checklist (deliveries `checklist`, for P-32).** `/results/storm-checklist-{UTC time as YYYYMMDDTHHMMSSZ}.md`: one Markdown line `- [ ] {channel}: "{first line}" with the link {L}` for every expected message of `SB-S05 incident messages` and `SB-S06 approval messages`, where `{channel}` is "Teams channel {team name}" (the team names of AR-I-31 and AR-I-22; for the three teams that get no message, NOC Shift Lead, NOC Management, Customer Care), "Teams channel Approvals — Usman" or "Teams channel Approvals — Farah" (SB-SET-06) for a Teams path, and "mailbox of {team name}", "mailbox of Usman Tariq (DB Ops)" or "mailbox of Farah Naqvi (DB Ops Lead)" for a mailbox; then `- [ ] no message in {channel}` for the six recipients of `SB-TL-05 no escalation`. The run's result file names it.
- **Result file.** `/results/storm-{mode}-{UTC time as YYYYMMDDTHHMMSSZ at the run's start}.json` (written also when the run fails), keys (complete list): `driver` (`"storm"`), `mode`, `deliveries`, `profile`, `started_at`, `finished_at`, `finished`, `t0`, `tenant_id`, `reset` (`branch_a_seconds`, `quiet_seconds`, `branch_b_seconds`, `total_seconds`), `incidents` (`storm` and `report`, each `incident_id`, `number`, `ticket_key`), `times` (seconds after T0, or null: `SB-TL-01` the latest storm `fired_at`; `SB-TL-02` the storm's `stable_grouping.stable_at` of AR-I-30; `SB-TL-04` the first storm incident message (`at` of the Teams call, `fakes` only); `SB-TL-06` the acknowledgement; `SB-TL-07` the first approval request message (`fakes` only); `SB-TL-08` the execution's `finished_at`; `SB-TL-10` `resolved_at`), `investigation` (`runbooks_cited`, `likely_cause`), `checklist` (its file name or null), `checks` (a list of `{id, name, passed, detail}`, the end entries of Section 6.2 included), `not_run` (every check of Section 6.3 not run because of the mode, the profile or the deliveries, as `{id} {name}`), `passed` (`finished` and every entry of `checks` passed). The times are recorded, not checked (CTX-D-083 (4)). The program prints one line per step and per check (`PASS` or `FAIL`, ID and name) and finally `result file {name}`. Exit code 0 when `passed`, 1 otherwise.

6. Exit 0 when the driver exited 0 and AR-Q-07 passed, else 1.
